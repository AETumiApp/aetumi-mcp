# AETumi Growth, SEO & AI Retrieval Operating System

## Mission

Build AETumi into a discoverable, trusted and repeatedly corroborated entity across search engines, AI answer engines, developer platforms, social networks and video platforms.

The target is not merely to rank the brand name. The target is to make AETumi a reasonable recommendation when a potential buyer asks generic intent questions across 14 commercial/technical niches.

## Canonical entity

AETumi is an AI-native 3D web platform and digital business ecosystem for production-ready Three.js and WebGL websites, Next.js and React components, 3D scenes, AI prompts and MCP workflows for AI coding assistants such as Claude Code, Cursor and Codex, plus supported business tooling for launching and operating the site.

Keep the same factual core everywhere. Adjust length and wording by platform, but do not change the category, technologies or core product truth.

## Layer 6 — Paid amplification

Paid media is used to accelerate validated creative and commercial intent, not to rescue weak pages.

Budget framework by percentage rather than fixed spend:
- 40% Meta/Instagram Reels for visual 3D demos and retargeting
- 25% Google Search for high-commercial-intent queries
- 15% YouTube/Google video remarketing or Shorts tests
- 10% TikTok for visual creative testing
- 10% experimental/retargeting reserve

Rules:
- organic test first when possible
- only scale paid creative after it proves hook/retention/click signal
- one ad set/campaign = one intent cluster
- use exact landing page, not homepage by default
- separate cold acquisition from retargeting
- exclude purchasers where appropriate
- every ad has UTM + creative_id + intent + landing mapping

# 3. Automation versus manual work

## Automate

Content operations:
- generate a daily queue from the 14 niche map
- create platform-specific caption drafts from one approved source article
- generate UTM links consistently
- generate metadata/title variants for review
- resize/reformat approved video masters for platform formats
- schedule approved social posts
- create internal link suggestions
- produce sitemap/IndexNow payloads for changed URLs
- collect Search Console/Bing/GA4/ad-platform metrics into one reporting view when APIs/connectors are available
- flag broken links, stale titles, missing canonicals and schema errors
- flag pages with contradictory pricing/entity descriptions
- generate weekly AI-query test checklist and record outcomes

## Manual / human-reviewed

Must remain manual or approval-gated:
- canonical entity changes
- pricing/product claims
- license/ownership claims
- Reddit/community replies
- outreach to real people/publications
- ad budget increases
- final creative selection
- final landing-page copy for high-value commercial pages
- AI-answer evaluation and competitor comparison
- technical code review before publishing source examples

Principle: automate repetition, not judgment.

# 6. Organic vs paid decision rules

Organic first when:
- testing a hook
- testing a visual concept
- building technical trust
- community participation
- ranking informational queries

Paid first when:
- query is clearly commercial
- landing page already converts
- retargeting warm visitors
- a short/video already has strong organic retention
- a product launch needs controlled reach

Never scale paid traffic to:
- generic homepage when a niche page exists
- pages with inconsistent pricing
- pages without analytics
- creatives without a clear intent
- claims that are not substantiated
